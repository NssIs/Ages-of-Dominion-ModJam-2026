## Medieval RPG music pack

The campaign score is selected from the **Medieval RPG music pack** by **playlightgames**:

- Pack page: <https://playlightgames.itch.io/medieval-rpg-music-pack>
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as stated on the pack page
- The pack page permits commercial use

The following eleven tracks are included. They were transcoded from MP3 to Ogg Vorbis for
Minecraft; no musical edits were made. Their measured combined duration is **3,588.88 seconds
(59:48.88)**, keeping the shipped score below one hour.
